Fonts shipped with this repository
==================================

Both faces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The
licence text is in OFL.txt alongside this file, and applies to both.

Jost — Jost-Light.ttf (300), Jost-Regular.ttf (400), Jost-SemiBold.ttf (600)
  Copyright 2020 The Jost* Project Authors
  https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost
  Static instances as served by Google Fonts.

  A geometric sans in the Futura tradition: circular bowls, single-storey a,
  uniform stroke. Futura is the mid-century typeface, which is why it suits
  the room the panel lives in. 600 carries every numeral and the headline,
  400 every label and sub-line, 300 the "/" between today's high and low and
  nothing else.

Weather Icons — weathericons.ttf
  Copyright (c) 2013 Erik Flowers
  https://erikflowers.github.io/weather-icons/

  Meteorological rather than cartoon, single colour, and a font — so it hints
  to the pixel grid at any size instead of being resampled from a PNG.
